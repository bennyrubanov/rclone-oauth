Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, Benny Rubanov, so that cloud jobs he runs can save and read back files in his own Google Drive.

It uses only Google's drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it created, and nothing else in his Drive.

The app and this website collect, sell and share no data with anyone. The Google sign-in token is kept in his own private storage. He can remove the app at any time in his Google Account, under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps & services".
